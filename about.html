About Kabankalan Catholic College

Learning shaped by faith and purpose.

A Catholic learning community in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental, where education supports growth in knowledge, character, and care for others.

Who we are

Education that sees the whole person.

Kabankalan Catholic College brings together learners, educators, and families around a shared belief in meaningful education. Guided by Catholic values, the college experience is about more than academic progress: it is also a chance to grow in confidence, responsibility, and compassion.

As part of the Kabankalan community, KCC celebrates learning as a lifelong journey—one that helps each person discover their strengths and put them to good use.
