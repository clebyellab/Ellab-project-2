Welcome to KCC

Education with heart, purpose, and possibility.

Kabankalan Catholic College is a Catholic learning community in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. Here, learning is an invitation to grow—not only in knowledge, but also in faith, responsibility, and care for others.

Inspired by the values of Catholic education, KCC brings together learners, educators, and families in a shared commitment to meaningful growth. Every day is an opportunity to ask questions, build skills, and discover how each person can contribute to the wider community.
